Moshka notes: image prompts for Abdelrahman or any provider.
Drop PNGs into: public/notes/moshka/images/
Filenames are case-sensitive. Lessons use figure(), ill(), or meme() helpers.

=== GLOBAL STYLE (append to every prompt) ===
Minimal black line art on cream off-white #F6F4EF. No photo realism. No gradients.
No purple, no neon green, no lime on white. Optional one cool grey or navy #2a3544 accent only.
Sharp corners. Lots of whitespace. Labels 1-4 words max. Self-explanatory at a glance.

=== MEME STYLE (for moshka-meme-*.png only) ===
Same GLOBAL colors. Simple 2-panel OR classic top text / bottom text meme layout.
Stick figures or minimal icons OK. Educational joke, not mean. Text baked into image, large readable sans-serif, black on cream.

=== DIAGRAM STYLE (for moshka-ill-*.png and loaders) ===
Same GLOBAL colors. Flow arrows, boxes, icons. Like a textbook figure, not a dashboard screenshot.
